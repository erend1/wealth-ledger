# WealthLedger Data Capture Guide

Status: Proposed canonical capture requirements

Last reviewed: 2026-09-16

## Purpose

This guide defines the source facts that user workflows and future importers
should capture. It is a product-level checklist, not a database schema. Domain
rules, accepted ADRs, and implemented use cases remain authoritative for exact
storage and arithmetic.

The central rule is:

> Record evidence-backed facts once; derive balances, values, gains, and
> allocation from them.

## Data classification

Every candidate field belongs to one of four classes:

| Class | Meaning | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Source fact | Appears on a statement, receipt, contract, physical item, or explicit user decision | Persist with provenance where useful |
| Classification | Adds stable economic meaning, such as contribution category or cost treatment | Persist as explicit stable vocabulary |
| Derived result | Can be reproduced from source facts, such as position or fine-gold weight | Calculate; do not make authoritative mutable state |
| Analysis or proposal | Assumption, forecast, target, or agent suggestion | Store separately from posted ledger facts |

Unknown evidence is not zero. A blank value must not be converted into a
fabricated amount, date, price, or acquisition cost.

## Common transaction facts

Capture these when applicable to every economic event:

- household;
- transaction type;
- order, execution, and settlement business dates;
- portfolio purpose;
- source and destination accounts;
- institution or counterparty;
- asset and quantity delta;
- cash consideration and currency;
- executed unit price and price currency;
- external source reference;
- human note;
- evidence reference or import provenance;
- household-member attribution when relevant;
- typed cost components and their treatments;
- creation and posting timestamps generated by the system.

The system generates internal identities and retry/idempotency metadata. Users
may inspect them diagnostically but should not need to invent them.

## Contribution

Required source facts:

- destination cash account and portfolio;
- currency asset;
- positive amount;
- contribution date;
- cash-flow category.

Optional facts:

- contributing household member;
- originating institution or description;
- external reference;
- note and evidence reference.

A contribution is external capital entering the ledger. It is not the purchase
of a fund or gold asset.

## Withdrawal

Capture the source cash account and portfolio, currency asset, positive entered
amount displayed as an outgoing effect, business date, category, reference,
note, and evidence. It is external capital leaving the ledger and is not a sale.

## Fund purchase

Capture:

- fund identity and lot-tracking policy;
- destination investment account and portfolio;
- source cash account;
- acquired unit quantity;
- execution date;
- total cash consideration;
- executed unit price and price currency;
- order and settlement dates when known;
- commission, tax, brokerage, or other costs with treatment;
- institution, external reference, note, and evidence.

Preserve both executed price and cash consideration as source facts. If their
mathematical relationship differs because of rounding, fees, settlement, or bad
input, show and resolve the discrepancy explicitly rather than overwriting one
with the other.

## Fund sale

Capture:

- fund and source account/portfolio;
- disposed unit quantity;
- execution and settlement dates;
- gross or net cash proceeds, with an explicit semantic contract;
- executed unit price and price currency;
- fees and withholding with treatment;
- lot-selection policy or explicit allocations when the accepted workflow
  requires them;
- institution, external reference, note, and evidence.

Realized cost is derived from lot allocations. It is not a user-entered sale
field.

As implemented by M008, the user does not select lots. The sale preview shows
the deterministic scope-aware first-in-first-out plan, and the post carries
that reviewed plan back so the server can refuse it if intervening history
changed which lots would be consumed.

Cost treatment fixes the cash effect exactly. Only `AdditionalCashOutflow`
creates a second cash entry:

    purchase net cash = -(consideration + additional outflow)
    sale net cash     = +(consideration - additional outflow)

`IncludedInConsideration` and `WithheldFromProceeds` are already inside the
entered consideration, and `InformationalOnly` never moves cash. The
acquisition lot records `consideration + additional outflow`, counting each
cost exactly once.

A Fund trade accepts only Commission, Brokerage, WithholdingTax, OtherTax and
Other, at most sixteen components, each with a positive amount in the trade
currency. A purchase rejects `WithheldFromProceeds` because it has no
proceeds. Every new trade requires an external reference or a note.

## Physical-gold purchase

M009 records one completed cash purchase as one homogeneous acquisition lot.
Capture:

- physical-gold asset or product identity;
- physical-vault account and portfolio;
- optional seller Institution, separately from custody;
- purchase date;
- authoritative exact gross raw-E8 weight;
- immutable actual fineness in integer ppm;
- positive original whole-piece count;
- total cash consideration and currency;
- source cash account and cash asset;
- optional observed executed price per gross gram, never an inferred per-piece
  or fine-gram price;
- making charge and whether it is included in consideration or an additional
  cash outflow;
- hallmark, certificate reference, product/form label, note, and evidence when
  available;
- other fees or taxes with treatment.

Derive fine-gold weight from gross weight and fineness. Do not duplicate it as
an independently editable authoritative field.

Gross quantity and pieces are independent evidence. A purchase allocation
stores both positive facts and creates exactly one Known-cost lot whose cost is
cash consideration plus additional cash outflow, counting included cost only
once. Seller is optional global Institution evidence; it neither owns the lot
nor replaces the selected PhysicalVault custody.

Preview preserves exact price/consideration discrepancies rather than
rewriting either fact. A difference beyond the accepted one-minor-unit rounding
tolerance requires explanatory provenance. Post revalidates the reviewed facts
atomically, stores a versioned command fingerprint, and returns the persisted
receipt on an equivalent retry.

Jewelry wording such as "no making-charge loss" is not a numerical source fact.
Actual executable buy and sell observations are market/reference data and must
be captured separately if spread analysis is desired.

## Physical-gold sale

Capture:

- each explicitly selected acquisition lot;
- exact positive gross raw-E8 quantity and exact positive whole-piece count for
  every selected lot;
- optional buyer Institution, separately from custody;
- sale date;
- cash proceeds and currency;
- optional observed execution price per gross gram;
- fees, taxes, assay deductions, or other deductions with treatment;
- reference, note, and evidence.

PhysicalGold sale never silently applies the Fund FIFO policy. Gross quantity
and piece movement are recorded independently with matching negative signs.
For a partial grouped lot, both facts must come from evidence or measurement;
never infer one from the other or divide original gross weight by original
pieces.

Preview derives availability in the selected Account/Portfolio custody scope
and returns a deterministic versioned plan fingerprint. Post re-derives gross
and piece availability inside the write transaction and refuses a stale plan
without writing. Realized cost extends ADR-009 over the selected lot's gross
raw-E8 disposal sequence, never its pieces. Unknown cost remains Unknown, and
mixed currencies remain separate completeness-aware buckets; sale capture does
not ask the user for realized cost, gain, tax, or valuation.

## Transfer

For a physical-gold transfer, capture distinct source and destination
PhysicalVault accounts, source and destination portfolio purposes, the
PhysicalGold asset, each explicitly selected acquisition lot, exact moved
gross raw-E8 quantity, exact moved whole pieces, transfer date, reference,
note, and evidence. Optional accepted transfer costs retain their explicit
treatment; the transfer itself has no consideration entry.

An internal transfer preserves total household quantity and acquisition
lineage. M009 writes equal-and-opposite quantity and piece allocations against
the same acquisition lot. It does not create income, return, a new lot, a new
cost basis, or a new acquisition price. Only an accepted
`AdditionalCashOutflow` transfer cost can produce a separate negative Fee/Tax
cash effect; informational cost does not.

Transfer preview and post use the same versioned stale-plan protection as sale.
Post checks the source custody again inside the write transaction and never
draws from another scope merely because household-wide lot quantity exists.

Purchase, sale, and transfer receipts are reconstructed from persisted facts
after redirect, retry, direct navigation, or restart. Correction never edits a
posted activity: it posts an exact reversal of entries, lot allocations, and
piece movement, leaves original costs and trade evidence on the original, and
records any corrected replacement through a separate normal reviewed command.

## Opening balance

M007 implements one explicit opening command for one household, portfolio,
account, asset, and as-of date. Capture:

- opening as-of date, which becomes the transaction ExecutionDate;
- account, portfolio, asset, and quantity;
- a positive exact currency amount, fund units, equity shares, or physical-gold
  gross weight;
- one or more exactly reconciled lots for funds, equities, and physical gold,
  including assets whose stored lot-tracking mode is Optional;
- known historical acquisition date only when evidenced, otherwise no date;
- Known historical total lot cost and currency only when evidenced;
- explicit Unknown cost when evidence is absent;
- physical-gold fineness, positive piece count, and optional hallmark,
  certificate reference, and lot note for each distinct lot;
- a required explanatory source note and an optional external reference.

Cash in the household base currency and foreign currency create no acquisition
lot. Their cost basis is NotApplicable; they do not create a contribution,
balancing counter-entry, or cash-flow classification.

For funds and equities, exact source lots may mix Known and Unknown cost. A
broker-supported aggregate total cost with unknown acquisition dates is one
aggregate lot with a null date and Known total cost, not an inferred unit
price. No opening entry carries UnitPrice.

Physical-gold pieces share one lot only when the selected product, fineness,
date knowledge/value, cost status and supported total, hallmark, certificate,
and provenance genuinely describe one evidence group. Gross weight is the
authoritative quantity. The UI maps labelled karat/fineness choices or an exact
0.001-to-1000 per-mille value to integer ppm and derives fine weight; the user
does not enter or persist fine weight separately.

The as-of date, optional historical lot acquisition date, and current UTC
posting timestamp are different facts. An acquisition date cannot follow the
as-of date, the as-of date cannot precede a known account opening date or lie in
the future, and the caller never supplies the audit timestamp.

Opening market value, when entered for inception analysis, remains a dated
valuation observation in a later workflow and must not masquerade as historical
cost. M007 neither asks for nor stores current value, gain/loss, or performance.

After review and posting, read the persisted transaction and derived position.
An incorrect opening is corrected with a separate reversal and a new opening;
never edit or delete the original. Once M010's API and UI slices expose it, an
evidence record (see "Evidence records") can retain the source and link it to
the opening. Neither the note/reference nor a linked evidence record proves
that an independent reconciliation occurred; only a reconciliation run (see
"Reconciliation evidence") records that comparison.

## Adjustment

Capture the exceptional reason, signed quantity effect, business date, account,
portfolio, asset, approving user, reference, note, and evidence. Adjustment is
not a convenience path for ordinary purchase, sale, transfer, or correction.

## Cost components

For each cost, capture:

- type;
- non-negative amount and currency;
- treatment;
- note or evidence when useful.

Treatments must answer whether the cost is an additional cash outflow, withheld
from proceeds, already included in consideration, or informational only. This
prevents double counting.

Spread is not a cost component. It is derived from comparable bid and ask
observations.

## Market and reference observations

Capture:

- instrument or asset;
- observation timestamp and effective business date;
- bid, ask, close, net asset value, exchange rate, or other explicit quote type;
- value, unit, and quote currency;
- source/provider;
- whether the value was manual, imported, or provider-supplied;
- freshness and quality metadata;
- correction or supersession relationship when a source revises data.

Do not overwrite a historical quote merely because a newer quote exists.

## Goal and policy facts

Capture separately from the ledger:

- goal name and purpose;
- target amount and currency;
- target date, date range, or explicitly flexible horizon;
- minimum liquid reserve;
- permitted asset universe;
- target allocation ranges and concentration limits;
- loss tolerance and risk-reduction schedule;
- review frequency;
- effective date and policy version;
- rationale and approval.

Changing a policy does not rewrite the policy that governed a historical
decision.

## Decision journal

A decision entry may capture:

- decision date and review date;
- decision owner;
- question being answered;
- alternatives considered;
- facts and valuation date used;
- assumptions;
- selected action or reason for no action;
- expected portfolio role;
- risk limits or invalidation conditions;
- links to later actual transactions;
- retrospective outcome and lesson.

An agent suggestion is labeled as generated analysis and remains unposted until
a human submits a validated command.

## Evidence records

An evidence record is retained external support for ledger facts. It is audit
data, not a ledger fact: recording or linking it never posts, edits or deletes
a transaction and never changes a balance. The rules below are enforced by the
Application and SQLite; the capture screens and endpoints arrive with M010's API
and UI slices.

Capture:

- source kind: Statement, Receipt, PhysicalCount, ContractOrCertificate, or
  Other;
- the source's effective date, which cannot lie in the future;
- a required single-line label of at most 256 characters;
- optional single-line reference of at most 256 characters and optional note
  of at most 2000 characters. None of the three may contain bidirectional
  control characters or broken Unicode;
- optional institution and household account. When both are given, the account
  must belong to that institution;
- zero or more links to Posted transactions of the same household; and
- optionally, one retained source file.

A retained file must be PDF, PNG, JPEG, UTF-8 plain text or CSV, at most
10 MiB. Its extension and content signature must match the declared type, and
text must not open with markup. SVG, HTML, scripts, executables, Office macro
formats and other binary content are refused before anything is stored. Only
the file name's last segment is kept, with unsafe characters replaced. The
application computes the SHA-256 and byte length itself; the digest proves
byte identity, not financial truth.

A record and its file are immutable. To correct one, record a new evidence
record that supersedes it; the original stays readable. A record can be
superseded once, and a superseded record takes no new links, observations or
reconciliation runs. Links and observations can be added later but never
removed. There is no evidence delete.

Recording is retry-safe under ADR-006. A retry with the same idempotency key
and the same content returns the original record without storing a second
copy; different content under the same key is a conflict.

Use synthetic files for tests and examples. Real statements and receipts belong
only in the owner's live database and its backups.

## Reconciliation evidence

A reconciliation compares what a statement or physical count observed with what
the ledger derives for the same scopes on the same date. It is audit data: it
never posts, edits or deletes a transaction and adds no Adjustment shortcut. The
rules below are enforced by the Application and SQLite; the capture screens and
endpoints arrive with M010's API and UI slices.

Observations belong to an evidence record, whose effective date applies to all
of them. Capture, per observed scope:

- the exact portfolio, account and asset. They must belong to the record's
  household and, when the record names an account or an institution, lie
  inside it;
- the observed quantity in the asset's base unit, signed, exact to eight
  decimal places (cash in currency units, Fund units, shares, gross grams);
- an explicit non-negative quantity tolerance in the same unit, zero by
  default; and
- for physical gold only, the counted whole pieces when the source states them.
  Piece tolerance is always zero.

A record holds at most 100 observations, one per scope. Repeating a stored
observation adds nothing; different facts for a stored scope are refused, and a
wrong observation is corrected by superseding the evidence record. A scope the
source did not cover is simply not observed: it is never treated as zero.

A run compares every observation of one record. For each, the ledger side is
the scope's effective Posted quantity on the evidence date, and for physical
gold the pieces in that scope's lot custody on that date. The run stores, per
item, the observed and ledger quantities, `delta = observed - ledger`, the
tolerance, and `Matched` only when the absolute delta is within the tolerance
and any piece delta is zero; otherwise `OutOfTolerance`. Backdated history can
make an as-of ledger quantity negative; it is compared as it is and shows up as
a difference. A run is retry-safe: the same idempotency key for the same
evidence returns the original run.

Each out-of-tolerance item stays `Open` until it is resolved once, by either:

- `CorrectedByTransaction`, linking a Posted transaction of the same household
  that was recorded through the normal workflows; or
- `DocumentedException`, with an explanation of at most 2000 characters.

A resolution never changes the item's delta or its `OutOfTolerance` status. To
see a correction take effect, run the reconciliation again: the new run compares
against the corrected history.

Opening a run derives its ledger side again. When later effective history, such
as a reversal dated on or before the evidence date, no longer reproduces the
stored ledger quantity or pieces, the run is shown as stale and a new run is
needed; the old run is never rewritten.

Never resolve a difference by editing a posted transaction in place.

## Derived values that users must not enter as truth

Do not create routine editable fields for:

- current balance or position;
- remaining lot quantity;
- average purchase price;
- current portfolio value;
- current profit or loss;
- current allocation percentage;
- fine-gold weight;
- realized cost basis;
- performance return.

These are query results with an effective date, method, and provenance.

## Units, precision, and boundary conversion

Human interfaces accept decimal-formatted values and convert them deliberately
to Domain representations. They must validate scale, overflow, sign, currency
precision, and rounding before posting. Binary floating point is never used for
authoritative financial conversion.

Importers must preserve the original source text or evidence reference when a
rounding or normalization decision could later matter.

## Minimum provenance rule

Every manually entered or imported transaction should be explainable later by
at least one of:

- institution and external reference;
- receipt or statement reference;
- physical inventory evidence;
- explicit opening-balance note;
- explicit adjustment rationale.

The application should support references without copying sensitive source
documents into source control or agent context.
